## What these checks do not claim

Syntax parsing is not a complete SFM semantic compilation or a live-machine test. It does not verify your label assignments, registry/tag contents, exposed slots and sides, transfer throughput, recipe behavior, or compatibility with moving-structure integrations. The PDF was generated and its page structure checked; individual printed pages were not all visually reviewed. Copy controls were checked for presence and their source text was verified; OS clipboard interoperability can vary between browsers.

The manual's notes about bare `IF REDSTONE`, condition-level `EXCEPT`, relative-side resolution, and the no-op redstone resource transfer methods are based on the pinned implementation. They are not presented as live-world reproductions.

The interactive RETAIN calculator is a simple model for one resource, one source, and one destination with sufficient capacity. It is not an implementation of the SFM runtime.

Validation was limited to documentation, parser checks, and browser behavior. No Minecraft client or server was launched for runtime testing.
